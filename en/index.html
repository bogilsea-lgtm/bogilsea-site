Philosophy

What is old is not worn out.

A four-hundred-year-old garden, island water, makgeolli slowly ripening, the patience of the seasons. We do not keep the old as it was; we brew it anew for today's glass.

Terroir

Makgeolli, brewed by Bogil Island's water and time

Water

Clean, mineral-rich island water from the Dadohae Marine National Park.

Rice

Good rice, chosen grain by grain. The rice sets the depth of everything that follows.

Time

Slow fermentation and aging, waiting for the seasons to turn. Bogilsea does not rush time.

Since 1671

Beside Seyeonjeong

On his way to Jeju, the poet Yun Seon-do (Gosan) was caught in a typhoon and landed briefly on Bogil Island. Taken by its beauty, he made the island his home. Seyeonjeong, the garden he built and one of Korea’s three great gardens, still stands on Bogil Island. 1671 is the year Gosan ended his life here. We carry that year forward, honoring his philosophy and spirit, and brew makgeolli beside Seyeonjeong.

FAQ

Questions

What is Bogilsea Brewery?

Bogilsea Brewery (보길도가) is a makgeolli brewery on Bogil Island in Wando County, South Korea. We brew with island water beside Seyeonjeong, the garden built by the poet Yun Seon-do. Our philosophy: reimagining the timeless.

What do you brew?

Three makgeolli: Bogilsea Makgeolli (9%), an everyday triple-brewed makgeolli; Bogil Gosan Makgeolli (12%), a clear makgeolli served in a wine glass; and Bogil Dongbaek (9%), brewed only as the camellias fall, 300 bottles a year.

How can I buy?

Send us a message on Instagram @bogilsea_brewery and we will share pricing and delivery options.

What is “wine makgeolli”?

It refers to Bogil Gosan Makgeolli: a clear, 12% makgeolli best served chilled in a wine glass and sipped slowly like a white wine.

Can I visit the brewery?

Yes, visits are welcome. Please get in touch beforehand and we will arrange a time. Message us on Instagram.
