The Story

The everyday of Bogil Island

Bogilsea Makgeolli is what islanders pour after a day at sea, mooring the boat, at the evening table. We brew it for ordinary days, not special ones.

Brewed in three stages (samyangju) and slowly fermented, it is deep and smooth, with a dry, clean finish — the taste of Bogil Island's sea itself.

Tasting Notes
Nose

Deep, calm notes of steamed grain.

Palate

Smooth and rounded, never overly sweet.

Finish

Dry and clean, calling you back for another bite.

How to Enjoy

How to Enjoy

Serve chilled in a generous cup. Gently turn the bottle to mix the sediment for a rounder taste.

Best with Korean dishes such as tofu and kimchi, jeon pancakes or boiled pork.
